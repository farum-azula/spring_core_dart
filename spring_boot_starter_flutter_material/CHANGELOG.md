## Unreleased

- First version: Material Design for `spring_boot_starter_flutter`, which knows no design library from this release on. Built on the Flutter team's `material_ui` (Flutter 3.50 deprecates `package:flutter/material.dart`).
- **Fixed:** a page that asks for `PageTransition.platform` showed no transition when the theme set no `motion.page-transition` (the theme's `none` made Material's route too), and the theme's kind when it set one. It now has the platform's own (Cupertino's slide on iOS and macOS, the predictive back on Android, the zoom on Windows and Linux), with the theme's duration and curve when it gives them. A `pageTransitionsTheme` that code sets (a `ThemeCustomizer`) is still Material's route's. Material's routes of the app's own follow the theme's `motion.page-transition` as before.
- The app widget of `spring.flutter.app.type: material`: a `MaterialApp` (`MaterialAppFactory`, the bean `materialAppFactory`), with the settings and themes it had in `spring_boot_starter_flutter` before.
- `MaterialThemeDesign`, among the module's factories: the palette of `seed-color`, `scheme-variant` and `contrast` (`ColorScheme.fromSeed`), the theme properties `components.*`, `visual-density`, `tap-target-size` and the ink `splash`, and the `zoom` page transition.
- **Moved here from `spring_boot_starter_flutter`:** `SpringThemeData.toMaterial()`, `visualDensity`, `tapTargetSize` and `splashFactory` (now the extension `MaterialSpringThemeData`, with `materialColorScheme`), `SpringThemeExtension`, `ThemeCustomizer`, and the component themes (`ThemeComponent`, `themeComponents`, `themeComponentNames`).
- **Changed:** `SpringThemes.material(name)` is `MaterialThemes.theme(name)`: the bean `materialThemes` has the themes as `ThemeData`, changed by the `ThemeCustomizer` beans; a `ThemeData` bean named `theme` or `darkTheme` still replaces the made `light` or `dark` theme.
- `MaterialThemeReader`: `density`, `inputBorder` and `splash` on the `ThemeReader` of a component.
- `MaterialPageRoutes` (the bean `materialPageRoutes`): Material's page route and hero flights for the router of `spring_boot_starter_flutter_navigation`.
- `MaterialDesignControls`: Material's text field and filled button for the widgets that draw a form without knowing the design (the login form of `spring_boot_starter_flutter_security`), given through the `DesignScope` of the app.
